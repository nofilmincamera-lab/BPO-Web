# Klarna Judgment Layer
## Designer-Ready Animation Storyboard

**Format:** Executive RFP animation with PDF-safe key frames  
**Recommended duration:** 55–65 seconds  
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9  
**Primary audience:** Klarna RFP evaluators across Operations, FinCrime, Risk, Procurement, and Transformation  
**Single message:** Klarna automates the clear cases. Foundever resolves defined exceptions. Klarna retains material authority. Every quality-accepted case gives Klarna structured insight that can make future automation smarter.

---

## Creative Direction

### What the viewer should understand

Within five seconds:

> Klarna automation handles the clear majority. Foundever works the cases that require judgment.

By the end:

> Foundever does not merely process exceptions. It resolves them through a governed operating model, captures structured insight, and gives Klarna better information for future detection, routing, and controls.

### Cognitive-load rule

The viewer follows **one case token through one stable six-step architecture**. The architecture remains fixed. The case moves.

Do not pan across a large workflow map. Do not introduce multiple simultaneous cases until the final “next time” proof. Do not display detailed queue mechanics, SLA clocks, or long evidence lists in the main animation.

### Visual grammar

- **Klarna automation and retained authority:** Indigo
- **Foundever L1, L2, and judgment work:** Mint
- **Threat or unresolved risk:** Coral
- **QA and accepted closure:** Dark navy with mint acceptance state
- **Learning loop:** Indigo-to-mint return path, explicitly labeled “Klarna-controlled learning”
- **Background:** Warm white

Color must reinforce ownership. Do not use color decoratively.

---

## Persistent Screen Architecture

Use one horizontal six-stage model across the screen:

1. **Klarna automation**
2. **Foundever L1**
3. **Foundever L2**
4. **Klarna retained**
5. **QA accepted**
6. **Klarna learns**

A curved return path connects stage 6 back to stage 1.

### Persistent elements

Keep these stationary throughout:

- The six stage labels
- Ownership colors
- The Klarna/Foundever authority boundary between stages 3 and 4
- The return-loop path from stage 6 to stage 1
- A small footer: **“Illustrative operating model based on Klarna RFP workflows”**

### Animated elements

Animate only:

- The case token
- Three evidence signals
- The delegated-authority branch
- The QA checks
- The structured learning signals
- One comparable “next case” at the end

## Opening Frame

### Time

**0:00–0:05**

### On-screen copy

**Headline**

> Inside Klarna’s judgment layer

**Subhead**

> One case. Six governed steps. A smarter next decision.

**Breadth marker**

> A common operating model across 13 RFP queues

### Visual

The six-stage architecture appears in its final location. Stage labels reveal from left to right in a restrained sequence. The return loop is visible but muted.

### Motion

- Fade in headline: 350 ms
- Reveal six stages: 60 ms stagger
- Hold complete model for approximately 1.5 seconds
- Introduce one case token at the left edge

### PDF-safe frame

The opening must work as a standalone title page with the complete six-stage model faintly visible beneath the headline.

---

## Stage 1: Klarna Automation Identifies the Exception

### Time

**0:05–0:13**

### Stage label

> 1 · Klarna automation

### Primary copy

> Clear cases continue. This case needs judgment.

### Visual action

A short stream of four or five small case tokens enters the automation stage.

- Most tokens pass through a clean path and fade.
- One token receives a subtle coral outline.
- Three compact signal labels briefly appear:
  - New device
  - Address change
  - Unusual basket
- The exception token drops into the Foundever judgment path.

### Motion intent

The animation must establish that Foundever does not touch the clean majority. Foundever begins where Klarna’s automation creates a defined exception.

### Avoid

- A dramatic alarm effect
- More than three visible signals
- Language implying that automation failed
- Language implying that the customer was declined

### Optional caption

> Held for review, not declined.

### PDF-safe frame

Show the clean path continuing and one highlighted exception routed to Foundever.

---

## Stage 2: Foundever L1 Triages the Case

### Time

**0:13–0:21**

### Stage label

> 2 · Foundever L1

### Primary copy

> Confirm the queue, evidence, and required action.

### Evidence labels

- Identity or device
- Account behavior
- Transaction context

### Visual action

The exception token enters an L1 case card. The three evidence labels converge into the card and organize into three aligned rows.

The card receives:

- Queue assignment
- Required evidence
- SLA indicator

These should appear as short labels or icons, not sentences.

### Motion intent

Show structure, not investigation depth. L1 turns an ambiguous alert into a correctly routed and complete case.

### Avoid

- Long checklists
- Generic call-center imagery
- A person/avatar becoming the visual focus
- Treating L1 as a separate permanent team rather than a certified activity level

### PDF-safe frame

Show one organized case card with the three evidence categories and a visible “L1 triaged” status.

---

## Stage 3: Foundever L2 Investigates

### Time

**0:21–0:31**

### Stage label

> 3 · Foundever L2

### Primary copy

> Resolve the ambiguity. Build the evidence-backed decision.

### Visual action

The L1 case card expands slightly. The three evidence rows resolve into:

- Two confirmed facts
- One remaining uncertainty

A decision panel appears with two governed paths:

1. **Within delegated authority**  
   Resolve with the lightest proportionate action.

2. **Material or policy-sensitive**  
   Prepare a complete Klarna approval pack.

The case in this animation selects the second path so the authority boundary can be demonstrated.

### Motion intent

Show that Foundever applies judgment inside a defined playbook. It does not invent policy or make unrestricted risk decisions.

### Suggested microcopy

> Evidence complete

> Recommendation documented

> Escalation trigger met

### Avoid

- A scoring dashboard with invented numerical precision
- A “fraud/not fraud” binary presented as the only outcome
- Making the analyst appear to change Klarna’s rules

### PDF-safe frame

Show the two decision paths, with the selected escalation path highlighted and the delegated path visible but muted.

---

## Stage 4: Klarna Retains Material Authority

### Time

**0:31–0:40**

### Stage label

> 4 · Klarna retained

### Primary copy

> Judgment can be delegated. Material accountability stays with Klarna.

### Retained-authority labels

- Policy interpretation
- Material-risk decisions
- SAR/STR filing authority
- Risk appetite

### Visual action

A clear vertical authority boundary becomes more prominent between Foundever L2 and Klarna retained.

Foundever’s completed evidence pack crosses the boundary. The visual should show the **pack** crossing, not Foundever taking the final authority with it.

Klarna reviews and confirms the decision. The case token then moves to QA.

### Motion intent

This is the governance proof. The viewer should immediately see where supplier authority ends.

### Avoid

- Suggesting Foundever directly files a SAR/STR in the base model
- Sending every case to Klarna
- Making Klarna look like a routine rework step
- Showing an ambiguous shared-ownership zone

### PDF-safe frame

The ownership boundary and retained-authority labels must be legible without narration.

---

## Stage 5: QA Accepts the Outcome

### Time

**0:40–0:48**

### Stage label

> 5 · QA accepted

### Primary copy

> The outcome is complete only when the decision, evidence, and rationale meet Klarna’s standards.

### QA checks

1. Correct decision
2. Complete evidence
3. Compliant rationale

### Visual action

The case passes through a restrained three-part QA gate.

- Each check resolves in sequence.
- The case receives a **Quality accepted** status.
- The case closes.
- The closed case remains visible long enough for the learning transition to originate from it.

### Motion intent

QA is not decoration and not an after-the-fact score. It is the gate between work performed and an accepted outcome.

### Avoid

- Confetti or celebratory motion
- A generic shield icon as the entire QA story
- Implying that every case must be manually QA-reviewed unless that is the proposed operating rule
- “Billable” language in the main executive animation unless commercial mechanics are intentionally in scope

### PDF-safe frame

The three QA criteria and final accepted status should form one self-contained still.

---

## Stage 6: Klarna Learns and Makes the Next Decision Smarter

### Time

**0:48–0:59**

### Stage label

> 6 · Klarna learns

### Primary copy

> The accepted case gives Klarna structured insight that can make automation smarter next time.

### Structured learning signals

Extract three labeled blocks from the closed case:

- Risk pattern
- Decision outcome
- Control effectiveness

### Visual action

1. The accepted case remains closed at stage 5.
2. Three small insight blocks lift out of the case.
3. They enter stage 6, labeled **Klarna-controlled learning**.
4. The blocks travel along the return loop toward Klarna automation.
5. The automation node subtly updates: its routing lines become clearer or a new pattern marker appears.
6. A second, similar case enters.
7. The second case is recognized or routed more precisely.
8. The second case should complete this moment quickly; do not begin another full workflow.

### Essential governance label

Place this beside the return path:

> Structured case insight informs future detection, routing, and controls.

### Motion intent

This is the strategic payoff. Foundever’s operation creates disciplined, reusable knowledge. Klarna controls how that insight is validated and applied to automation.

### Required wording discipline

Use:

- “Informs”
- “Can improve”
- “Klarna-controlled learning”
- “Makes the next decision better informed”

Avoid:

- “The model automatically retrains”
- “Foundever updates Klarna’s model”
- “Every case changes the algorithm”
- Any unqualified claim of autonomous production-model learning

### PDF-safe frame

The curved loop from **Klarna learns** to **Klarna automation** must be visible and labeled. The viewer should understand the feedback mechanism from a single still.

---

## Final Hold

### Time

**0:59–1:03**

### Headline

> The case closes. Klarna learns. The next decision gets smarter.

### Supporting line

> Thirteen queues. One governed judgment model.

### Visual

Show the complete six-stage architecture in its settled state:

**Klarna automation**  
→ **Foundever L1**  
→ **Foundever L2**  
→ **Klarna retained**  
→ **QA accepted**  
→ **Klarna learns**  
↩ **Future detection, routing, and controls**

### Brand treatment

- Foundever logo: lower left
- “Prepared for Klarna”: lower right
- No additional statistics
- No new capability claims

### Hold duration

Minimum 3 seconds so the frame can be read, captured, or used as a PDF page.

---

## Voiceover or Caption Script

If voiceover is used, keep it close to the following:

> Klarna’s automation handles the clear majority. When a case needs judgment, it enters a defined Foundever queue. L1 confirms the evidence and required action. L2 resolves the ambiguity or prepares a complete escalation. Policy interpretation, material-risk decisions, and filing authority remain with Klarna. The outcome closes only when its decision, evidence, and rationale meet Klarna’s quality standards. Then the case creates structured insight: what pattern appeared, what decision worked, and whether the control was effective. Klarna can use that insight to improve future detection, routing, and controls. The case closes. Klarna learns. The next decision gets smarter.

If no voiceover is used, the primary copy in each stage must remain on screen long enough to read comfortably.

## Motion Specifications

### Timing

- Standard element entry: 300–400 ms
- Case movement between stages: 450–600 ms
- Evidence-item stagger: 50–70 ms
- QA sequence: approximately 300 ms per check
- Learning-loop travel: 1.2–1.6 seconds
- Final hold: minimum 3 seconds

### Easing

- Entries: fast-start, gentle-settle ease-out
- Movement between visible stages: ease-in-out
- Exits: faster than entries
- Progress along the learning loop: smooth and continuous

### Motion hierarchy

The case token is always the primary moving object. Supporting labels may fade or reveal, but they must not compete with the case.

### Accessibility

- Respect reduced-motion settings
- Never rely on color alone; use stage labels and ownership names
- Keep all body copy large enough for presentation viewing
- Ensure every animated sequence has a complete settled state

## PDF Companion Frames

The animation should yield six deliberate stills rather than random video captures:

1. **The operating model**  
   All six stages and the breadth statement

2. **Exception routing**  
   Klarna automation routes one defined exception to Foundever

3. **Foundever judgment**  
   L1 structures the case; L2 resolves or escalates

4. **Retained authority**  
   The explicit boundary between Foundever and Klarna

5. **Quality-accepted closure**  
   Decision, evidence, and rationale pass QA

6. **Klarna learning loop**  
   Structured insight returns to future detection, routing, and controls

Each still should have:

- One conclusion-led title
- One dominant diagram
- No more than three supporting labels, except retained-authority terms
- A short “what this proves” line

## Production Checklist

### The animation is ready when

- A viewer understands the proposition within five seconds.
- The architecture remains stable while the case moves.
- Foundever is visibly absent from the clean automated path.
- L1 and L2 have distinct purposes.
- Klarna-retained authority has a hard visual boundary.
- QA appears before closure.
- Stage 6 explicitly says Klarna learns something from the accepted case.
- The learning loop shows insight returning to Klarna-controlled automation.
- The second case demonstrates smarter recognition or routing without starting a new story.
- Every major beat has a clean, printable settled frame.
- No frame requires the viewer to read operational minutiae to understand the point.

### Final comprehension test

Show the final frame to someone for five seconds, then hide it. They should be able to say:

> Klarna automates the straightforward cases, Foundever handles governed exceptions, Klarna keeps the important decisions, QA validates the result, and the learning improves future automation.
